At FRANKIE & HUNTER, we believe crafting is about so much more than the things we make.

For me, it has always been about slowing down, being creative and spending meaningful time with the people I love.

When my children were young, the kitchen table became our creative space — filled with paper, paint, glue, imagination and plenty of laughter.

We made crafts, but more importantly, we made memories.

I wanted them to know that something handmade carries a little piece of the person who made it — a simple way of saying I thought of you or you matter to me.

Today, that tradition continues with my grandchildren, with creative sleepovers, parties and usually a little bit of mess.

That feeling is at the heart of Frankie & Hunter.

We want to make crafting welcoming, inspiring and enjoyable, whether you're an experienced maker or just discovering the joy of creating something yourself.

We hope you'll find something here that inspires you to create, give with love and make wonderful memories along the way.

Most of all, we're looking forward to getting to know you, discovering what you love to make and becoming a little part of your creative story.
